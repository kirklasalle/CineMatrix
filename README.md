## 🌟 Executive Overview

**Storyboard AI** is a professional-grade generative cinema pre-visualization, directing, and publishing ecosystem. It ingests standard Hollywood screenplays, comic scripts, manuscripts, and literary works across 9 universal file formats, orchestrating multi-agent AI pipelines to produce:

- **16:9 Broadcast-Grade Visual Storyboards** with automated camera and lighting specifications.
- **24 FPS Sub-Pixel Motion Animatics** with camera transforms (Dolly In/Out, Pan, Handheld, Slow-Mo).
- **Blake Snyder 15-Beat Sheet Coverage** with real-time narrative pacing arcs.
- **Multi-Voice Virtual Table Reads** with synchronized teleprompter and dialogue attribution.
- **300 DPI Print-Ready Graphic Novels** with geometric balloon placement and manga split layouts.
- **Procedural 44.1kHz Audio Scores & Foley SFX** across 6 musical genres and physical sound effects.
- **Master Production Export Suites** (16:9 PDF Keynote Decks, Shot List CSVs, ZIPs, Fountain, and Master MP4 Video Reels).

## 🎬 The 8 Studio Engines

<div align="center">

![Storyboard AI 8-Engine Suite Showcase](docs/images/storyboard_engines_suite.jpg)

</div>

### 1. 🖼️ Storyboard Discovery & Visual Synthesis
- **Auto-Action Image Generation**: Automatically synthesizes rich 16:9 cinematic storyboard visuals during live script streaming with zero delay.
- **Cinematography Specification Bar**: Permanent, high-contrast display of **Shot Type** (CU, WS, OTS, Dutch Angle), **Camera Motion** (Dolly, Tracking, Crane), **Lens** (35mm Anamorphic, 50mm Prime, 85mm), **Lighting** (Chiaroscuro, Golden Hour, Volumetric), and **Director's Note**.
- **Batch Master Actions**: 1-click *Render All Frames* with concurrency and fallback resilience.
- **Fullscreen Lightbox**: High-resolution image inspection with scene notes.

### 2. 🎥 Multimodal 24 FPS Video Animatic Suite
- **Sub-Pixel Motion Rendering**: Pure local OpenCV affine camera motion engine generating broadcast-quality 24 FPS HD video clips (`.mp4`).
- **6 Camera Motion Presets**: *Dolly In (Push)*, *Dolly Out (Pull)*, *Cinematic Pan Left*, *Cinematic Pan Right*, *Handheld Camera Tension*, and *Slow-Motion Ambient Drift*.
- **Master Video Reel Compiler**: Stitches all scene animatics into a single master video reel with director title cards and scene transition slates.
- **Cinema Video Player**: Loop playback, full controls, and direct download.

### 3. 🩺 AI Story Doctor & Blake Snyder 15-Beat Sheet
- **Narrative Beat Sheet Mapping**: Maps screenplay scenes to Blake Snyder's 15 structural beats (*Opening Image, Catalyst, Debate, Break into Two, Midpoint, All is Lost, Climax, Final Image*).
- **Dynamic Tension Curve**: Calculates and plots scene-by-scene dramatic intensity to identify pacing flatlines.
- **Studio Coverage Generator**: Instant executive script coverage summary with Logline, Commercial Viability Rating, Act Summaries, and Polish Recommendations.
- **Interactive Story Doctor Assistant**: Conversational agent for punch-ups, dialogue subtext, and scene surgery.

### 4. 👥 Character Consistency Library & Casting Look-Book
- **Deep Jungian Archetype Taxonomy**: Profiles named characters across 8 Jungian archetypes with wardrobe and visual signatures.
- **Visual Appearance Consistency**: Automatically injects character appearance constraints into visual generation prompts across scenes.
- **Model Sheet Matrix & Turnarounds**: 4-angle turnaround prompts (Front, 3/4, Profile, Back) and 6-expression facial matrices.
- **Executive Look-Book PDF**: Generates executive landscape PDF look-books with character bios and wardrobe specs.

### 5. 🎙️ Virtual Table Read Studio
- **Synchronized Dialogue Teleprompter**: Auto-scrolling script reader with live line-by-line character highlighting.
- **Voice Auditions**: Audition dialogue lines across 6 neural vocal profiles (`alloy`, `echo`, `fable`, `onyx`, `nova`, `shimmer`).
- **Speech Cadence Timing**: Natural human pacing model (~150 WPM) with dramatic pause calculation.

### 6. 📖 Graphic Novel & Comic Creation Studio
- **Multi-Panel Layout Engine**: Dynamic support for 1-Splash, 2-Cinematic, 3-Strip, 4-Quadrant, 6-Grid Manga, 9-Panel Watchmen, and Webtoon scroll layouts.
- **Speech Balloon & Caption Geometry**: Non-occluding balloon placement algorithm with tail directional vectors.
- **300 DPI Print Publisher**: Generates CMYK-ready print PDF files and standardized `.cbz` comic book packages.

### 7. 🎵 Procedural Cinematic Audio Scoring & Foley Studio
- **Procedural Sound Synthesis**: 16-bit 44.1kHz stereo audio engine generating mood-adaptive atmospheric music stems.
- **6 Musical Mood Presets**: *Cinematic Epic*, *Noir Melancholy*, *Synthwave Thriller*, *Orchestral Drama*, *Horror Drone*, *Tactical Action*.
- **Physical Foley Sound Effects**: Procedural generators for *Thunderstorm*, *Gunshot Reverb*, *Footsteps*, *Iron Door Creak*, and *Cinematic Braam*.

### 8. 📦 Master Presentation Export Suite
- **16:9 Keynote Storyboard PDF**: Broadcast-quality presentation deck with full camera specifications.
- **Production Shot List CSV**: Direct export for 1st ADs, DPs, and production managers.
- **Rendered Images ZIP**: High-res frame archive with CSV manifest index.
- **Round-Trip Fountain Screenplay**: Clean industry-standard screenplay files compatible with Final Draft and Highland.
- **Master Animatic Reel MP4**: Full stitched video reel.

## 🎨 Cinematic Styles & Genre Vaults

### 16 Visual Art Styles:
1. **Oscar Prestige (`oscar_prestige`)**: 35mm film grain, ARRI Alexa LF color science, subtle halation.
2. **Cyberpunk Neo-Noir (`cyberpunk_neon`)**: High-contrast cyan/magenta split, anamorphic lens flares, wet reflective asphalt.
3. **Nordic Noir (`nordic_noir`)**: Desaturated muted tones, overcast natural lighting, bleak high-latitude daylight.
4. **Vintage Noir (`vintage_noir_1940`)**: Black and white, deep chiaroscuro shadows, venetian blind key lights.
5. **Anime Cinematic (`anime_cinematic`)**: Makoto Shinkai / Studio Ghibli inspired skies, painterly watercolor backgrounds.
6. **Classic Graphic Novel (`graphic_novel_ink`)**: High-contrast ink lines, dramatic cross-hatching, stylized shadows.
7. **Hyper-Realistic Film Still (`hyper_realism`)**: Photorealistic 8K RED Monstro texture, shallow depth-of-field.
8. **Manga Kinetic (`manga_kinetic`)**: Dynamic speed lines, bold screentone shading, expressive action poses.
9. **Watercolor Storyboard (`watercolor_storyboard`)**: Soft washes, organic paper texture, expressive brush strokes.
10. **Retro 70s Cinema (`retro_70s`)**: Warm amber cast, Kodak Vision3 tone curves, vintage prime lenses.
11. **Grimdark Fantasy (`grimdark_fantasy`)**: Desaturated earth tones, gritty metallic textures, torchlight accents.
12. **Pastel Romance (`pastel_romance`)**: Soft golden-hour glow, low contrast, dreamlike bloom.
13. **Modern Blockbuster (`modern_blockbuster`)**: Vibrant teal & orange color grading, crisp digital lighting.
14. **Silent Era Expressionist (`silent_expressionist`)**: German Expressionism, distorted architectural angles, stark contrast.
15. **Concept Art Matte Painting (`concept_art_matte`)**: Broad cinematic vistas, atmospheric perspective, epic scale.
16. **Architectural Blueprint (`blueprint_minimal`)**: Clean cyan line drawings, technical draftsmanship, minimalist grids.

## 🚀 Quick Start

### Prerequisites
- **Python 3.10+** (Python 3.11/3.12 recommended)
- **Node.js 18+** & **npm**
- **Git**

### 1-Click Desktop Launch (Windows)
Double-click [`start_storyboard.bat`](start_storyboard.bat) or run in PowerShell:
```powershell
./start_storyboard.ps1
```
The launcher automatically installs dependencies, configures database tables, boots the backend on `http://127.0.0.1:8000`, starts the Vite frontend on `http://localhost:5173`, and opens your default browser.

### Manual Launch

**1. Start Backend:**
```bash
cd backend
python -m pip install -r requirements.txt
python main.py
```

**2. Start Frontend:**
```bash
cd frontend
npm install
npm run dev
```
