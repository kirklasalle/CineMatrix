PROJECT: UNDERCOVER STEEL: VOICES FROM THE LINE
GENRE: NATURALIST DOCUMENTARY / CINEMA VERITÉ
RECOMMENDED STYLE: DOGME / NATURALIST DOCUMENTARY

INT. GARY STEEL MILL - BLAST FURNACE FLOOR - NIGHT
CAMERA: Handheld 16mm gritty texture, available industrial light only. Focus breathes as camera operator tracks worker movement through orange sparks.

SOUND: Deafening mechanical rumble of rolling mills, hiss of high-pressure steam, distant klaxons.

EDDIE KOWALSKI (58, third-generation steelworker, soot-streaked face, reflective visor pushed back) wipes sweat from his forehead with a welding glove.

INTERVIEWER (O.S.)
Eddie, thirty-five years on the number four furnace. What happens to this town when they shut the burners down on Friday?

EDDIE
(looking directly into the lens, unblinking)
You see that glow behind me? That's two thousand degrees of molten iron. It don't care about stock dividends or offshore restructuring. For fifty years, that furnace paid for every hospital, every Little League field, and every college diploma in this valley. When it goes cold... this valley goes cold with it.

Eddie turns back to his crew as a fresh ladle of liquid steel tips into the casting mold, sending a fountain of golden sparks into the ceiling rafters.
