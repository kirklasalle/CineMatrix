MIDNIGHT CROSSING

                       Written by
                Storyboard AI Showcase Team

EXT. BERLIN WALL - CHECKPOINT CHARLIE - NIGHT

Snow falls in thick, silent clumps. Searchlights sweep back and forth across the barbed wire perimeter.

KLAUS (50s, heavy wool coat, leather briefcase handcuffed to his wrist) stands in the shadows of a bombed-out bakery. His breath plummets in white vapor.

Across the asphalt, an unmarked black sedan waits with its headlights dimmed.

KLAUS
(checking his watch)
Three minutes past midnight. Where is the courier?

From behind a rusted barrier, GRETA (30s, sharp features, beret) emerges. She holds up a silver lighter and flicks the flame three times.

KLAUS (CONT'D)
(exhaling)
Code verified.

Klaus sprints across the no-man's-land toward the vehicle. A SIREN erupts from the eastern watchtower. SEARCHLIGHTS converge upon them.

GRETA
Run, Klaus! Get in the car!

The engine ROARS as Greta throws the door open.
