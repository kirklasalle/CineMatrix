THE WINTER SOLSTICE

A Play in Two Acts
by Storyboard AI Showcase Team

CHARACTERS:
ARTHUR PENDLETON (60s) - Master of Greyfriars Manor.
ELEANOR PENDLETON (30s) - His estranged daughter, an astronomer.
THOMAS (70s) - The faithful estate steward.

SETTING:
The drawing room of Greyfriars Manor, North Yorkshire. A roaring stone fireplace right. A massive brass astronomical telescope positioned before french doors left, looking out onto a snow-swept moor.

TIME:
December 21, 1892. Twilight.

ACT I

SCENE 1

[AT RISE: The wind howls across the moor. ARTHUR stands by the fireplace, staring into the embers with a sealed parchment in his hand. THOMAS enters quietly from stage right carrying a silver tray with hot tea.]

THOMAS
(softly)
The London coach has arrived at the gates, Sir Arthur. Miss Eleanor is walking up the drive.

ARTHUR
(without turning)
Alone?

THOMAS
Alone, sir. She carried her own specimen cases through the snow.

[The heavy oak doors stage left SWING OPEN. ELEANOR enters, shaking snow from her heavy wool cloak. Her cheeks are flushed red with the winter gale.]

ELEANOR
Ten years, Father. And you still keep the fires burning only when company is expected.

ARTHUR
(turning slowly)
You are not company, Eleanor. You are the heir to Greyfriars. Whether you prefer the stars or the soil.

ELEANOR
(stepping toward the telescope)
The stars do not lie about our family's debts, Father. I have looked at your ledgers.

[BLACKOUT.]
